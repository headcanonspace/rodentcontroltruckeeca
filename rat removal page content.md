## H2: Roof Rats and Norway Rats in Truckee

Two kinds of rats show up in Truckee homes, and they don't nest the same way.

| Rat Type | Also Known As | Where It Nests | What Gives It Away |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Norway Rat** | Brown rat | Burrows near foundations, under decks, or in crawl spaces | Larger droppings, burrow holes near the base of a house, gnaw marks low on walls |
| **Roof Rat** | Black rat | Attics, upper walls, and tree cover close to the roofline | Sounds from above at night, droppings in the attic, nests in insulation |

Roof rats tend to show up in homes with trees close to the roof. Norway rats show up more in older construction, where the foundation has more gaps than a newer build.

### H3: How Do I Know If It's a Rat and Not a Mouse?

Rat droppings are about the size of a grain of rice or larger, while mouse droppings are much smaller, closer to a grain of pepper. Rats also chew larger gaps, often over an inch wide, while a mouse can fit through a gap the size of a quarter inch.

## H2: Why Rat Problems Get Worse After the First Freeze

Cold weather opens up new ways for rats to get inside a house. In neighborhoods like Glenshire, where homes sit closer to open land, heavy snow can push a roof board loose or bend a foundation vent soon after the first freeze, and a rat finds that gap fast. Once it settles in, it leaves a scent trail every time it comes and goes, and that trail is what pulls more rats in as winter goes on. A gap that looks small in October can turn into a full nest by January if it never gets closed.

## H2: Frequently Asked Questions

### H3: What Is a Humane Way to Get Rid of Rats?

Close the entry point first, then use traps instead of poison. This keeps the rat from dying inside a wall and keeps pets and kids safe too.

### H3: Can DIY Methods Be Effective for Rat Control?

A store bought trap can catch a rat now and then, but it won't find the nest or the gap the rat used. Rats avoid new objects too, so traps often sit untouched for weeks.

### H3: How Do I Find Effective Rat Removal Services?

Pick a company that checks the house first, tells you what it found, and seals the gap instead of only setting traps. Ask what happens if the rats come back.

### H3: Are Rats in Truckee Dangerous to Health?

Yes. Rats can spread illness through droppings, urine, and bites. That's why cleanup after a rat problem should include sanitizing, not just trapping.

### H3: What Diseases Do Rats Carry?

Rats can carry hantavirus, salmonella, and leptospirosis. All three spread mainly through droppings, urine, or a bite.
