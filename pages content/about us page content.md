# H1: About Rodent Control Truckee

We do one job: getting mice and rats out of Truckee homes. Not ants, not spiders, not every pest you might find in a mountain town. Just the two that cause the most damage and the most calls in this area.

---

## H2: Why We Exist

Most pest companies treat rodents as just one item on a long list. We built this service around one idea instead. Truckee winters cause specific problems. Snow pushes roof boards loose. Foundation vents bend under the weight. Gaps open and close every time it freezes and thaws. Handling that well takes focus, not a technician juggling ten different pests in the same week.

---

## H2: What Makes Us Different

### H4: Rodents Only, Not a Generalist

We work on mice and rats. That means the person at your property has spent real time learning how a Norway rat digs and how a roof rat climbs, not just how to set a general trap.

### H4: Snow-Rated Materials Built for Truckee Winters

We close entry points with steel mesh or metal flashing, not foam. Foam breaks down after one season of snow and freeze. A rodent can chew through it just as easily as it chewed in the first time.

### H4: No Poison, Trapping and Sealing Instead

We skip poison bait in most homes. A rodent that dies inside a wall or attic leaves a smell that can last for weeks. Trapping and sealing the gap solves the problem without that risk.

### H4: Photo Documentation for Remote Owners

Every job comes with photos of what we found and what we fixed. If you own a second home near Martis Camp or run a rental between guests, you can see the work without being on site.
