Roof Rats and Norway Rats in Truckee

Two kinds of rats show up in Truckee homes, and they don't nest the same way.

Rat Type Also Known As Where It Nests What Gives It Away
Norway Rat Brown rat Burrows near foundations, under decks, or in crawl spaces Larger droppings, burrow holes near the base of a house, gnaw marks low on walls
Roof Rat Black rat Attics, upper walls, and tree cover close to the roofline Sounds from above at night, droppings in the attic, nests in insulation

Roof rats tend to show up in homes with trees close to the roof. Norway rats show up more in older construction, where the foundation has more gaps than a newer build.

How Do I Know If It's a Rat and Not a Mouse?

Rat droppings are about the size of a grain of rice or larger, while mouse droppings are much smaller, closer to a grain of pepper. Rats also chew larger gaps, often over an inch wide, while a mouse can fit through a gap the size of a quarter inch.

Why Rat Problems Get Worse After the First Freeze

Cold weather opens up new ways for rats to get inside a house. In neighborhoods like Glenshire, where homes sit closer to open land, heavy snow can push a roof board loose or bend a foundation vent soon after the first freeze, and a rat finds that gap fast. Once it settles in, it leaves a scent trail every time it comes and goes, and that trail is what pulls more rats in as winter goes on. A gap that looks small in October can turn into a full nest by January if it never gets closed.

How We Remove Rats in Truckee

A trap in the garage might catch one rat, but it does nothing about the gap that let it in. If that gap stays open, another rat finds it within a season. That's why we don't stop at trapping, we find the entry point and close it too.We start by checking the attic, crawl space, foundation, and roofline for burrows, droppings, and gnaw marks, and we figure out which type of rat it is before setting anything.

We set traps where the rat is actually moving, not just where you saw it. We skip poison, since a rat that dies inside a wall can leave a bad smell for weeks. Once the rat is gone, we close the gap with steel mesh or metal flashing.

How Long Does Rat Removal Take?

A single rat or a small nest near a foundation can often be cleared within three to five days. A roof rat problem in an attic, especially with an existing nest, can take one to two weeks, since sealing a roofline gap takes longer than sealing a foundation gap.

Protecting Second Homes and Rentals from Rats

A roof rat is a good climber and mostly moves around at night, so it can settle into an attic for weeks before anyone hears anything, especially in a place that only gets checked once in a while. By the time an owner visits, the rat may have already chewed through insulation or wiring without a single sound to give it away during the day. We take photos as we go, so an owner who wasn't there can see exactly what a rat left behind and what it took to fix it.

Signs of a Rat Problem in Truckee

  • Scratching sounds in the walls or attic, usually worse at night
  • Droppings the size of a grain of rice, near baseboards or in the garage
  • Chew marks on wood, wires, or boxes
  • A hole near the foundation or under a deck
  • A greasy mark along a wall where a rat keeps walking the same path

Frequently Asked Questions

What Is a Humane Way to Get Rid of Rats?
Close the entry point first, then use traps instead of poison. This keeps the rat from dying inside a wall and keeps pets and kids safe too.
Can DIY Methods Be Effective for Rat Control?
A store bought trap can catch a rat now and then, but it won't find the nest or the gap the rat used. Rats avoid new objects too, so traps often sit untouched for weeks.
How Do I Find Effective Rat Removal Services?
Pick a company that checks the house first, tells you what it found, and seals the gap instead of only setting traps. Ask what happens if the rats come back.
Are Rats in Truckee Dangerous to Health?
Yes. Rats can spread illness through droppings, urine, and bites. That's why cleanup after a rat problem should include sanitizing, not just trapping.
What Diseases Do Rats Carry?
Rats can carry hantavirus, salmonella, and leptospirosis. All three spread mainly through droppings, urine, or a bite.
