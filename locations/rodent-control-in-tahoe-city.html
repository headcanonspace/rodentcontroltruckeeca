Signs You Need Rodent Control in Tahoe City

Identifying the signs of a rodent infestation early can save you from costly damages. Here are key indicators that you may need Rodent Control in Tahoe City.

  • Droppings near food sources

    Finding droppings near your pantry or kitchen indicates that rodents are actively foraging for food and may be nesting nearby.

  • Gnaw marks on furniture or walls

    Rodents gnaw on various materials to keep their teeth trimmed. Marks on furniture or walls suggest an infestation.

  • Nesting materials found in hidden areas

    If you discover shredded paper or fabric in hidden places, it likely indicates that rodents are nesting in your home.

  • Strange noises at night

    Hearing scratching or scurrying sounds at night may mean that rodents are active in your walls or attic.

  • Unpleasant odors

    A musty smell can indicate a rodent infestation, as their urine and droppings can create unpleasant odors.
